Kantahulay · Android karaoke player

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how Kantahulay (com.kantahulay.app) handles information when you use the app or contact its developer. For privacy questions, contact marcuis0ha@gmail.com.

Information collection and sharing

Kantahulay plays its bundled karaoke library offline. The app does not require an account, collect personal information for the developer, or send your song searches, queue, or settings to the developer or an app-operated server.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, or automatic crash-reporting services. We do not sell personal information or share app usage information with advertisers.

Information stored on your device

The app uses local app storage and a SQLite database to provide its features. This includes the song catalog, song reservations and queue state, language preference, tutorial completion status, audio volume, effects settings, Bluetooth lyric timing adjustment, and library preparation information.

This information supports playback and remembers your preferences. Song searches are processed on your device, not sent to an online search service. The app does not maintain an online user profile.

Audio and device access

Kantahulay generates and plays karaoke audio. It does not record your voice or access the microphone, camera, contacts, or device location. It uses Android audio focus and audio-route information to manage playback and respond to interruptions or disconnected headphones and speakers.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Preferences and library preparation information remain in app storage until changed, reset, or removed. The app clears song reservations when it initializes a new playback session after a fresh launch; ordinary backgrounding and resuming preserve the current session.

You can remove the app's local data through Android Settings by selecting Kantahulay and clearing its storage, or by uninstalling the app. Clearing storage removes saved preferences and local app state and requires the app to prepare its bundled library again. There is no app account to delete and no developer-hosted copy of your queue or settings.

Android backup is enabled in the app configuration. Depending on your device, operating system, and backup settings, Android or your device provider may back up or transfer app data. Such copies are controlled by that provider, not the Kantahulay developer. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may not remove existing backups; manage them through your device or backup provider's settings. Restoring a backup may restore saved app data.

Data handling and security

Local data is stored in the app's Android app-specific storage, subject to Android's application isolation and your device's security controls. Keep your device protected and up to date. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

When you contact support

If you email marcuis0ha@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve reported issues, and retain it only as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. Email is processed by the email service providers involved.

Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address; provider-controlled backups or legally required records may remain subject to their retention requirements.

This policy webpage

This page contains no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by this page, or embedded third-party content. When you visit the hosted page, its hosting provider may process technical request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the website. That processing is separate from the offline Android app and is subject to the hosting provider's privacy practices.

Changes and contact

If the app's information-handling practices change, we will update this policy and its last-updated date. For questions about this policy or your information, email marcuis0ha@gmail.com.
